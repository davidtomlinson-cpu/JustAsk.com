2. What the service does

JustAsk lets you ask us to source and purchase items on your behalf, so you don't have to. In short:

  • Ask — you tell us what you need: a description of the item, how many, where it should go, and when you need it by.
  • Source — our team (or our sourcing tools) looks for real, currently available options that match what you asked for.
  • Quote — we send you back up to three price options (Basic, Standard, Premium) for the item, and, where relevant, separate pricing for how quickly you need it delivered (Same Day, Next Day, or a date you choose).
  • Deliver — once you choose an option and pay, we arrange for the item to reach you and keep you updated on its progress.

We act as principal in this transaction: when you pay us, you are paying JustAsk directly for the item and our service together, not paying a third-party retailer through us. We then separately purchase the item from wherever we have sourced it.

3. Making a request

You can submit a request either as a guest (tracked only on the device you used to submit it) or by creating a free account (so you can track requests from any device and receive email updates). Either way, you'll need to give us enough detail — what you want, where it should go, and by when — for us to source and quote it properly.

Submitting a request is not a binding order and does not commit you to anything. It simply asks us to find out what your item would cost.

4. Quotes and pricing

4.1 Budget tiers

For most requests, we'll come back with up to three price options — Basic, Standard, and Premium — reflecting different quality or specification levels we've found for the item you asked for. You choose whichever suits you; you are never charged for an option you didn't select.

4.2 Delivery speed

Where relevant, delivery speed (Same Day, Next Day, or a specific date you choose) is priced separately from the item itself, and is also your choice. Same Day requests are best-effort only — we'll always try, but cannot guarantee same-day delivery is possible for every item, postcode, or time of day, and requests submitted very late in the day may not be achievable same-day at all.

4.3 How our pricing works

The price we quote you includes both the cost of the item and our fee for sourcing, arranging, and managing the purchase on your behalf. We do not separately itemise our margin.
